Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Med Timer does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Your data stays on your device

The medications, doses and settings you enter are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Notifications

Dose reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They do not pass through any server.

Sharing

If you use the Share button on the History screen, the dose log is passed to the app you choose (for example Mail or Messages). What happens next is up to you and that app.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Erase All Medications and History in the app, or delete the app. Either removes everything Med Timer has stored.

This website

medtimer.app uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other companies' servers. The web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Children

Med Timer does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions? Open an issue.

Med Timer is a reminder tool, not medical advice. The intervals in its medicine list are common starting points only. Always follow your prescription, the label, and your doctor or pharmacist.
